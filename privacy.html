Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

Health Connect Data: With your explicit permission, WalkCoins reads your daily step count from Google Health Connect to reward you with in-app coins. We do not access, write, or store any other medical or fitness data (e.g., heart rate, weight, etc.). Your step data is processed securely to calculate rewards and is not shared with third parties.

Account Data: We collect your email address or phone number for account authentication via Supabase. We also store your in-app coin balance, reward history, withdrawals, and referral data (such as users you have successfully referred).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your step data is exclusively used to grant you daily rewards and update your position on public campaigns and leaderboards (which only show your chosen username). Your referral and withdrawal data (including manual fulfillment of gift cards and physical rewards) is used solely to process your rewards and prevent fraud.

3. Account Deletion and Data Retention

You may request full account deletion at any time directly within the WalkCoins app by navigating to Settings > Legal & Policy > Delete Account.

  • Deleted Immediately: Your login credentials, email/phone, device identifiers, app preferences, and daily step/activity history are permanently deleted.
  • Anonymized / Retained: To maintain the integrity of our financial ledgers, your historical records (withdrawals, gift cards, physical rewards, and campaign participations) are anonymized. They are permanently stripped of your identity and reassigned to a secure, inaccessible Sentinel ID. We do not retain any way to link this data back to you.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Health Connect data to third parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have any privacy questions, please contact our support team at support@walkcoins.internal.
